Read with care

  • Key latency isn't compared. The benchmark could time 25 of 108 presses on the old home and 1 on the new one (it only sees frames the home draws), so there's no fair per-key number. Judge the feel.
  • Jank % isn't comparable either: the old home redrew constantly for its auto-playing banner; the new one only draws when you move.
  • Cold start is the home app starting from nothing; as Home it stays loaded, so it rarely matters.
  • Per-process memory “before” is from the 18:44 snapshot; “after” from 20:40. Apps you opened (Netflix, Settings) come and go between the two.
